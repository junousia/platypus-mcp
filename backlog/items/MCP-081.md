# MCP-081 Direct work preparation semantics

## Goal

Remove ambiguity around `prepare_work` for `direct_edit` items by making it clear whether direct preparation is persisted, inspectable, or response-local guidance.

## Implementation Contract

Choose and implement one durable product behavior for direct preparation. Either persist a lightweight direct-prepared marker that queue/session inspection can report, or explicitly document and return that direct preparation is not persisted and that `complete_backlog_item` is the next durable transition. Keep worker handoff preparation unchanged.

## Acceptance

- `prepare_work` output for direct items states exactly what state was or was not persisted.
- Queue or item inspection no longer makes a prepared direct item look misleadingly unchanged.
- Generated host guidance includes the direct prepared follow-up path.
- Tests cover the direct prepare response and the subsequent inspect behavior.
