# MCP-090 Clarify direct prepare response semantics

## Goal

Make direct_edit prepare_work outputs machine-readable and less surprising by exposing that no durable preparation state is persisted.

## Implementation Contract

Add compact structured fields to direct prepare responses, such as state_persisted=false and durable_next_tool=complete_backlog_item, and reduce repetitive prose while keeping recovery guidance clear.

## Acceptance

- prepare_work direct_edit responses expose whether state was persisted and the durable next tool in structured data.
- inspect_work_queue and generated guidance explain that direct_ready remains visible until complete_backlog_item records closure.
- Direct prepare output avoids repeated response-local warnings while still preventing worker/worktree confusion.
- Tests cover direct prepare response data and guidance text.
